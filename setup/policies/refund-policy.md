## 1. 7-day refund window

You can request a **full refund within 7 days of your purchase date**, for any reason. You do not need to
justify the request, and you do not need to have attended any class.

To request a refund, email **[EMAIL]** or message **[WHATSAPP NUMBER]** with:

- the email address you used at checkout, and
- your order number (it looks like `#1001` and is in your confirmation email).

## 2. How long it takes

- We confirm every refund request within **2 business days**.
- Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method**. Banks and payment gateways typically
  take **5–7 business days** (UPI and cards) to show the credit. Some card issuers take up to 10 working days.
- We refund the **full amount you paid**, including any taxes collected. We do not deduct processing fees.
- Refunds are processed in Indian Rupees. If you paid in another currency, your bank's exchange rate on the
  refund date applies, and your bank may charge its own conversion fee.

## 3. When a refund is not available

- **After the 7-day window.** Once 7 days have passed from the purchase date, the sale is final. This is
  because the course is delivered immediately and includes lifetime access to recordings and materials.
- **Repeated purchases and refunds** from the same person or payment instrument.
- **Chargebacks filed without contacting us first.** Please write to us before contacting your bank; we
  resolve nearly every request within two business days.
- **Accounts that shared or resold course access**, or abused the community, in line with our
  [Terms of Service](/policies/terms-of-service).

## 4. Cancellation by us

If we cancel a cohort, or you cannot be placed in the cohort you paid for, you may choose either a **full
refund** or a **seat in the next cohort** at the same price. We will email you before making that choice.

If a live class is postponed, that is not a cancellation — the class is rescheduled and you are notified by
email and WhatsApp.

## 5. Missed classes

Every live class is recorded and shared within 24 hours, so missing a class is not grounds for a refund.
You keep lifetime access to all recordings.

## 6. Damaged, wrong or undelivered access

Course access is emailed automatically after payment. If you have not received your access email within
**12 hours** of a successful payment (check spam and promotions first), contact us and we will resend it
immediately. If we cannot deliver access at all, you get a full refund.

## 7. GST invoices

A tax invoice in the name you enter at checkout is emailed with your order confirmation. Need it changed?
Email **[EMAIL]** with your GSTIN within 30 days of purchase.
