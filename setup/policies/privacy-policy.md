[LEGAL BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us") operates [STORE URL]. This policy explains what personal data we
collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It is written under the Digital Personal

By buying a course, joining a waitlist or contacting us, you agree to this policy.

## 1. What we collect

| What | Why | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Name | To identify you in class and on your invoice | Yes |
| Email address | Order confirmation, class links, recordings, support | Yes |
| Phone number | Delivery updates and WhatsApp support (if you message us) | No |
| Billing city, state, PIN code, country | Tax invoices and payment verification | Yes |
| Payment details | Taken directly by our payment gateway. **We never see or store your full card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials.** | Yes |
| Order history | Refunds, support and access records | Yes |
| Course activity (attendance, class recordings access, community messages) | To run the cohort and improve it | Yes |
| Device and usage data (IP address, browser, pages viewed, referring site) | Security, fraud prevention, and understanding what is working | Automatic |

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. The course is for adults.

## 2. Why we use it

- **To deliver what you bought** — sending class links, recordings, templates and updates.
- **To take payment and meet tax law** — issuing GST invoices and maintaining accounting records.
- **To support you** — answering questions by email or WhatsApp.
- **To improve the course** — understanding which classes and materials help most.
- **To market to you** — only if you opted in, and every message has an unsubscribe link. You can also
  reply STOP on WhatsApp.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary, and only with processors who are bound to protect it:

- **Shopify** — hosts the store, processes orders and takes payment (servers may be outside India).
- **[PAYMENT GATEWAY, e.g. Razorpay / Stripe / PayPal]** — processes your payment.
- **[VIDEO PLATFORM, e.g. Google Meet / Zoom]** — hosts live classes; your display name and email may be visible to the class.
- **[EMAIL TOOL, e.g. Klaviyo / Shopify Email]** — sends access, recordings and updates.
- **WhatsApp Business (Meta)** — if you contact us on WhatsApp.
- **[COMMUNITY PLATFORM, e.g. WhatsApp Group / Discord / Circle]** — hosts the student community.
- **[ANALYTICS / ADS, e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel]** — aggregate measurement and advertising, if enabled.
- **Government or law enforcement** — where we are legally required to share.

**We never sell your personal data.** Delete any row above that you do not actually use.

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep your cart and session working, and analytics cookies to count
visits. Marketing cookies (if enabled) only load after you accept them in the cookie banner. You can clear
or block cookies in your browser; checkout may stop working if you block the strictly necessary ones.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Order, invoice and tax records | 8 years (statutory requirement) |
| Account and course access records | While the course exists, then 2 years |
| Class recordings containing your voice or video | Until the recording is retired from the portal |
| Marketing contact details | Until you unsubscribe |
| Support conversations | 2 years |

## 6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

- **See** the personal data we hold about you, and how it is processed.
- **Correct** anything inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Erase** data we no longer need for a legal purpose. Note that we must keep invoice records for tax.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time — for example, by unsubscribing from emails.
- **Nominate** someone to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
- **Complain** to the Data Protection Board of India if you are unhappy with our response.

To use any of these, email **[EMAIL]** with the subject "Privacy request". We respond within **30 days**.
There is no charge.

## 7. How we protect it

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in access-controlled systems. Access is limited to the people who
need it. No system is perfect — if a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the Data Protection
Board as required by law.

## 8. International transfers

Some processors (for example Shopify and our email tool) store data outside India. Where that happens, we
rely on the processor's contractual safeguards and transfer only what is needed to run the store.

If we change this policy we update the date above, and email you about material changes before they apply.
